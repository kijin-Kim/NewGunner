This SFX pack contains sounds that are useful for Valorant edits and montages.
Valorant is currently in beta, so the contents of this pack may change if they update sounds.
Keep in mind this SFX pack focuses on sounds for edits and montages, so I wont add every little sound like I did with Fortnite.
That being said, I know there are sounds I have not added, so please make requests and I'll add them in a future update.
Contact me on Twitter or leave a comment on the original video for missing SFX you need in your edits.

The changelog below shows detailed updates to this SFX pack.

============================================================================================================================================================================
						UPDATES
============================================================================================================================================================================
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11 MAY 2020 | Valorant SFX Pack (v0.49)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Released new SFX Pack

	- Agents: Voice lines (VO) and abilities of each agent
		- VO filenames roughly represent the actual voice line
		- Abilities include Equip, Cast, Loop, Deploy, De/activate, etc.

	- Announcer: Every voice line from the announcer
		- Organized based on the VO subject: Spike, Agent, Rounds, Time, Multikill, etc.
		- Pretty sure some of these aren't even used in-game but have fun with them I guess
		- Unlike Agent VO, Announcer VO filenames are literally word-for-word what the announcer says

	- Kills: Music for multikills, and sfx for player hits (body and headshot)

	- Maps: Map-specific sfx for objects found during gameplay
		- Ascender (Ropes)
		- Ultimate Orb
		- Portal
		- Spawn Barrier

	- Spike: Plant, Countdown, Defuse
		- Includes sfx elements and mixes

	- UI: Menu and text-related sfx
		- Character Select
		- In Game: Spike, Victory, etc.
			- UI events like Thrifty, Team Ace or Clutch all use the same sound
		- Out of Game: Other UI SFX such as rank-ups, match found, XP gains, etc. 

	- Weapons: Every weapon in the game
		- Each weapon includes Equip, Reload, Shot
		- For now the gunshots are all mixes but in a future update I will split them into sfx elements to add variety
		- I don't have Reaver skins so I can't get SFX mixes for them. Identifying most sfx elements from the game files is difficult as well.
			- I'll maybe add them in another update.

	- NOTE: Many sounds are mono. That's just how they are in the game files. Changing them to stereo would only make the files larger for no reason.

	- NOTE: Sounds named sfx A, sfx B, etc. mean they are a sequence of sounds
		- Example: Casting Omen's ultimate plays a sequence of two separate sounds (A & B). Pair them together for the full sound effect.

	- NOTE: Sounds with 'Layer' in the filename means you place them on top of the other related sound to achieve the full sfx
		- Example: Sova's shock bolt is a mix of any 'Shock Bolt #' and 'Shock Bolt Layer'. Place any two together for full sfx.
			- Audio may have to be normalized when mixing with layers

	- NOTE: Sounds with 'Element' means they are a small sfx piece of a larger sfx (Except for Out of Game UI sfx, I simply don't know what to name them because I don't know their source)
		- Example: Brimstone's Orbital Strike equip isn't just one sound, it's a combination of various small sounds that are layered and sequenced to create the full mix
		- Example: 10 elements were sequenced and layered in a specific way just to make the spike plant sfx. I doubt I perfectly recreated it which is why it might sound a bit different from in-game.
